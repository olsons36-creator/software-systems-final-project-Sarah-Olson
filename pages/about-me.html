About Me


I'm Sarah Olson. I'm currently a student at Western Techical College. I have 2 cats and a dog who are all girls. Their respective names are Zoey, Daisy, and Mabel. Zoey is 11 years old and is the boss of the house. She's the oldest of the 3 and has been around the house the longest. Zoey loves begging for treats even though she has plently of food (I think she just likes treats better). She will allow me to pick her up, but she doesn't like it for too long. When I'm sitting down, she might come up and sit next to me. Whenever my family has people over, Zoey likes to supervise them.


Daisy is 8 and is the youngest. She acts like a baby and is very lazy. Whenever my family has people over, she runs away and hides either upstairs or in the basement. She likes playing with strange things like tennis balls, Q-tips, and hairties. If I start a jigsaw puzzle, Daisy will get on the table and sit on the puzzle. She might even steal the pieces and chew on them. Both cats sit on the puzzle, but Daisy does it the most.


Mabel is my family's first dog and she's big. She is around 90 pounds and is black lab with a little bit of Great Pyreneese. Mabel gets walked twice a day (unless it's really cold outside). When we got her, Daisy was still a kitten, so Mabel acted like Daisy's mother a little bit. Daisy would come up to her and Mabel would clean Daisy. Mabel was originally from Alabama, but she ended up in Wisconsin. One would think that she hates the cold, but she absolutely loves it. In fact, she actually gets really tired in the heat, but is really energized in the cold.
